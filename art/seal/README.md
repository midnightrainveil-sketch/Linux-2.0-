# The lock screen's wax seal

One seal seen from above on plain white, made with an image model and saved
here as `seal.webp`. To use another, save it here and run:

    python3 tools/import_seal.py art/seal/seal.webp

It is cut from the white (its shadow kept), centred and written to
`dotfiles/.config/quickshell/assets/seal/seal.png`. The lock screen pours,
presses and breaks it (modules/lock/WaxSeal.qml); the ribbon is drawn there,
so leave it out of the picture.

## Prompt

> A single red sealing-wax seal photographed straight from above (top-down,
> 90°), square image, centred, filling about 80% of the frame, on a plain
> pure white background. Deep madder-red wax, slightly glossy, with an uneven
> organic blob edge and a thick rim of wax squeezed out around the stamped
> area. The stamped impression is a circle with a fine beaded border and, in
> the centre, a delicate botanical sprig (a slender stem with paired leaves
> and a small five-petalled flower at the tip), crisply raised. Soft light
> from the top left, a soft shadow on the lower right. Realistic, high
> detail. No text, no letters, no ribbon, no string, no paper, no other
> objects.
