Everything on this desktop, where to find it, and how to work it. This note comes with the theme and is renewed by every ultra-update (you cannot change it; write your own notes beside it).

SUPER is the Windows key.


━━ THE GADGETS ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Super + Space      APP LAUNCHER (Kairo cartridges)
    Your apps are cartridges on a rail. Wheel or arrows to choose, Page Up / Down five at a time, just type to search, Enter (or a click on the one in the slot) to launch: the taskbar's port slides under the slot and the cartridge is lowered into it.

Super + C          ORGANIZER (this)
    CALENDAR, AGENDA, TASKS, NOTES. Start typing anywhere to add (an event; a task in Tasks; a note in Notes). Enter opens, Space ticks a task, Delete removes, Tab or the keys below the screen change page, the rocker steps, the round key is "new", the side wheel scrolls. In the editor: Tab / ↑ ↓ move, ← → change, type digits for the time (1230), Enter saves, Esc leaves. Reminders arrive on the pager even when the organizer is put away.

Super + R          RESEARCH STATION (typewriter)
    Type a question, Enter: the web's results (DuckDuckGo). ↑ ↓ choose one, Enter or a click opens it in your browser (and marks it READ). PULL OUT / PUSH IN on the tablet (or the round knob, or F11) slides the tablet out of its slot for big work and back; Esc pushes it in too. Tab or F1–F5: RESEARCH, SOURCES (the results by address), NOTES (per session), ARCHIVE (Enter opens, Delete removes), EXPORT (Markdown to ~/Documents/Research; Ctrl + S from anywhere). Its keys, springs, platen knobs and carriage move as you type.

Super + V          CASSETTE VENDING MACHINE
    Every song in ~/Music is a cassette. The lift is the list's slider: drag it to run through them all (top: the first song, foot: the last). Wheel / arrows / the side wheel step one at a time; type to search, Enter (or SELECT / EJECT) to dispense: it goes into the player and plays.

Super + A          CASSETTE PLAYER
    Plays the dispensed cassette, or (none in) whatever is playing elsewhere: a browser's video, a music app. PLAY (again to pause), REW / FF (tap: back / on, or the track before / after; hold: wind), STOP/EJ (stop; again: eject), REC opens the vending machine. Keys: Space play / pause, ← → ten seconds (hold to wind), ↑ ↓ volume, S stop, E eject, Home to the start, V vending. The knob is the volume (wheel or drag).

Super + W          CLOCK
    Time, date, an alarm and a timer; they ring even with the clock put away. Also: click the taskbar's time.

Super + S          CONTROL DECK
    Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (each opens its own list), power mode, refresh rate, lock time, volume and brightness faders, battery. Also: click the taskbar's Wi-Fi, volume or battery.

Super + Shift + Esc   POWER
    Lock, log out, sleep, restart, shut down: turn the roller (wheel or arrows), Enter. Also: the power button, or the Arch logo on the taskbar.

Super + N          NOTIFICATIONS (the KAZE pager)
    A notification slides in from the right edge and out again; swipe it to the right to send it away at once (it is kept). Tap the screen to open it, right-click or CLEAR to clear it, ◀ ▶ for older ones, ALL CLR for all. Super + N brings it up to look through (arrows, Enter, Delete, Shift + Delete, Esc).
Super + Shift + N  clear every notification
Super + Alt + N    Do Not Disturb, on / off (they are kept, quietly)

Every gadget: Esc or a click outside puts it away; swiping to another page does too (what it set going, like music, keeps going).


━━ THE DESK ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The picture moves: the city slides past the train window. The cassette player in her hands turns its reels and lights its meter while music plays.

The small terminal on the picture is part of the desk, not a window.
    Super + D      bring it back to its place
    Super + F      (on it) a fullscreen view of the same shell; close it and the small one is still there
    clear          resets it (and any terminal window) to its clean greeting; over SSH or in your own tmux it is the plain clear

The taskbar hides under open windows: push the pointer to the bottom edge to bring it up. Its port shows the cartridge of the app under the pointer.

━━ PAGES (WORKSPACES) ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Super + 1 … 9                  go to page
Super + Shift + 1 … 9          take the window there
Super + Tab                    the page you were on
Super + Ctrl + ← →, Page Up / Down     previous / next page
Super + Shift + Ctrl + ← →     take the window along
Super + wheel                  through the pages
Three-finger swipe             through the pages
Four fingers up / down         the volume, slowly, like a fader (1% at a time)
Super + `                      open the drawer (a hidden page)
Super + -                      put the window in the drawer
Super + Shift + `              take it out again

━━ KEYS ON THE G14 ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Volume, mute, mic mute, brightness, keyboard light: their own keys (the volume shows in amber at the right edge, the brightness in blue at the left)
Play / pause, next, previous: the media keys (any player)
Fan key             next power profile (quiet / balanced / performance)
ROG key             ROG Control Center
Power button        the power menu (hold to shut down)

━━ SETTINGS ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

~/.config/ultra/retro-futurism/settings.json, for example:
    "music": "~/Music"            where the cassettes come from
    "researchRegion": "us-en"     the research station's search region (default: everywhere)
    "cursorSize": 28              the pointer's size
    "deskTerminal": true          the small terminal on the picture
Log out and in after changing it.

Where things are kept: ~/.local/state/ultra/retro-futurism (the organizer, the research sessions, the notifications, the clock).
